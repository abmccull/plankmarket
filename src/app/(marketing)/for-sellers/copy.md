## Section 7: Pricing Transparency

**Section Title**

Know what you keep before you accept.

**Headline**

No listing fee. Pay when a sale completes.

**Description**

PlankMarket charges a 5% seller fee on completed inventory sales, calculated on
the inventory subtotal only. Sellers also pay Stripe processing on the
inventory subtotal. Freight is quoted separately before payment.

**Fee Example**

- Inventory sale: $12,000
- Seller fee (5%): $600
- Seller Stripe fee: $348.30
- Projected seller transfer: $11,051.70

## Section 8: FAQ

**Q1**

Question: What types of inventory can I list?

Answer: Sellers can list surplus, overstock, discontinued, and closeout
flooring inventory across the supported categories when condition is accurately
described.

**Q2**

Question: How does business verification work?

Answer: Sellers can create an account and explore the platform immediately.
Verification approval is required before publishing listings, and the review
uses submitted business details plus supporting documentation.

**Q3**

Question: Can I set minimum lot terms?

Answer: Sellers can define listing terms such as whole-lot or split-lot options
when the workflow supports them.

**Q4**

Question: When do I get paid?

Answer: PlankMarket initiates the seller transfer after confirmed carrier
pickup and the configured delay. Stripe payout timing to the bank depends on
the connected-account schedule.

**Q5**

Question: What if a buyer disputes the order?

Answer: Buyers can report issues through the order with supporting evidence,
and the platform keeps the payment, freight, and communication trail attached
for review.

**Q6**

Question: Is there a listing limit?

Answer: Publishable claims about listing limits or Pro entitlements should be
verified against the current product before use.
